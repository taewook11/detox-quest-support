Deep Detox / App Block on Android

Detox Quest requires Android Accessibility access for Deep Detox and App Block. Open Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps → Detox Quest app blocking and enable the service.

Some side-loaded development builds may also require Android’s Allow restricted settings option from the Detox Quest app-info screen.

Deep Detox / App Block on iPhone

Detox Quest uses Apple Screen Time / Family Controls on supported iOS builds. Make sure Screen Time access has been approved for Detox Quest. If access was revoked, reopen Detox Quest and follow the setup prompt.

Scheduled Focus

Scheduled Focus is a Detox Quest Plus feature. If a schedule does not activate, confirm that Detox Quest Plus is active, the required Android Accessibility or iOS Screen Time permission is enabled, and the schedule itself is enabled.

Ending a Focus Session

Soft Focus can be ended through the Give Up flow. Hard Focus intentionally adds additional friction before an emergency exit. Ending a session early does not earn the normal completion reward.

Purchases / Detox Quest Plus

If a subscription or purchase is not recognized, use Restore Purchases in Detox Quest. Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Detox Quest does not receive your full payment-card number.

Resetting Detox Quest

The app includes Reset All Game Data for removing local progress and settings. Resetting local data does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription.
