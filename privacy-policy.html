Detox Quest Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Detox Quest (“Detox Quest,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a digital focus and app-blocking application published by Famapp. This Privacy Policy explains how Detox Quest handles information when you use the app.

1. Information Detox Quest Uses

Detox Quest is designed so that most app data stays on your device. The app may store information locally, including focus-session times and duration, scheduled focus settings, selected Android app package names for App Block, privacy-preserving iOS Family Controls selections, in-app rewards and progress, preferences, and identifiers needed to prevent duplicate rewards or recover interrupted sessions.

2. Android Accessibility Service

On supported Android devices, Detox Quest uses the Android Accessibility Service to detect which app is currently in the foreground so that Detox Quest can block access to apps during an active Deep Detox or App Block session.

Detox Quest does not use the Accessibility Service to read or store typed text, passwords, messages, email content, screen contents, taps, keystrokes, Accessibility node text, browsing history, or a history of which apps you opened.

For App Block, selected Android package names may be stored locally on your device so the app knows which apps you chose to block. Accessibility access is optional and can be disabled in Android system settings.

3. iOS Screen Time / Family Controls

On supported iOS devices, Detox Quest uses Apple’s Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks to provide Deep Detox, Scheduled Focus, and App Block features.

For App Block, Apple provides privacy-preserving opaque selection tokens. Detox Quest stores those selections locally in the app’s shared App Group so blocking can continue when the main app is not running. Detox Quest does not attempt to convert those tokens into bundle identifiers, does not create an app-usage history, and does not store Screen Time reports or website-history data.

4. Focus and Reward Data

Focus sessions, reward eligibility, Gems, companion progress, daily limits, and related game state are primarily stored locally on your device. Local session identifiers and timestamps may be used to restore active sessions, determine successful completion, prevent duplicate rewards, and enforce daily reward limits.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Detox Quest uses RevenueCat to support and verify in-app purchases and Detox Quest Plus subscriptions. RevenueCat may process information needed to provide purchase and subscription services, such as app-user or device identifiers, purchase history, subscription status, store transaction information, and diagnostic information.

Payment information such as your full credit-card number is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play and is not provided directly to Detox Quest.

6. Advertising

Free users may be offered optional rewarded advertisements. Detox Quest uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for these ads. Google and its advertising technologies may process information such as device or advertising identifiers where permitted, IP address, app and device information, ad interactions, diagnostics, fraud-prevention information, and consent or privacy choices.

Detox Quest does not use Android Accessibility Service data or iOS Screen Time selections for advertising or ad personalization.

7. How Information Is Shared

Detox Quest does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers only as needed to provide their services, including Apple App Store and Google Play for distribution and purchases, RevenueCat for subscription and purchase management, and Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded advertising.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Detox Quest data can be removed using the app’s Reset All Game Data option or by deleting the app from your device. Some purchase, subscription, advertising, or transaction information may be retained by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or other service providers according to their own policies and legal requirements.

Detox Quest currently does not require users to create a Detox Quest account.

9. Children

Detox Quest is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures to limit the information Detox Quest handles and to keep core focus and blocking data on the device where practical. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. International Users

Detox Quest may be made available in multiple countries. Third-party providers such as Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their own privacy terms and applicable safeguards.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Detox Quest features, service providers, or legal requirements change. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and provide additional notice when required.
